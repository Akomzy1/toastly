Height, star sign, and children

Height is optional, picked in cm or ft-in. Star sign is off by default and worked out from date of birth. Choosing 1, 2 or 3 or more children brings up a note about who you'll be shown to. Children and "Do you want children?" each have a "Who can see this" choice. "Open to dating someone who has children?" sits on its own, private card below Family. On the full profile, height and star sign appear as quiet chips with the other details; neither appears on the Today's six card.
