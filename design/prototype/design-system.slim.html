03 — Marks & iconography

Three proprietary marks

The system has no swipe card, no flame, no generic video-call glyph. Three marks do the differentiating work and recur on every page.

Verified Real seal

A notched champagne seal, not a blue tick. Phone + selfie liveness is mandatory; NIN/BVN adds a second ring. Appears at 16px inline, 24px on profiles, 72px as a section mark.

Gist waves

Two voices, facing each other — gold for you, champagne for them. Structured voice-first sessions. Never a camera or a phone handset; video is a premium state shown by a third outer arc.

The Journey arc

A rising, dotted path with four stations that ends on a star, not a heart: Match → Gist → Couple Mode → AriyaPlanner. The arc is the brand's structural signature and always gets its own full-width band.

05 — Motion, imagery, accessibility

Rules of restraint

Motion

Section reveals: 24px rise, 0.7s, cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.44,1), staggered 80ms. Hover: 1px border shift or a 1px lift over 200ms. The Verified seal draws its check once on first view. Nothing bounces, nothing spins, no card ever flies off screen. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Imagery direction

Real Nigerian couples in real Nigerian light: Lekki balconies at golden hour, Abuja rooftops, aso-oke and agbada at introduction ceremonies, danfo-yellow and ochre walls. Warm skin tones held true, never blue-filtered. Photos are graded toward the green/champagne palette at 90% opacity over ink. The photos in this prototype are placeholders — commission local photographers in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Ibadan; never license generic "diverse couple" stock.

Weight & access

Two fonts, seven weights, no icon library, no hero video — the whole system is SVG and type. Every page keeps one h1 and a sequential heading order, 44px minimum touch targets, alt text on every image, and 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body copy.
