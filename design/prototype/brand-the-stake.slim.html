Brand system · Direction G

The Stake

Two coin rings overlapping above a line: what it looks like when both people put a coin down. The mark comes from the deposit mechanic, not from drinking — the overlap is two people meeting halfway, and the line is the table they both showed up to.

Toastly

Construction

Built on a 48-unit grid

Rings

Radius 9.5, stroke 3, centres at x19 and x29 — a 10-unit offset, so the overlap is a third of each ring. Never nest them tighter or pull them apart.

Line

32 units wide, 2.4 tall, fully rounded, sitting 4.5 units below the rings and centred on the mark. It drops out only at favicon scale.

Clear space

Keep one ring radius clear on all sides. In the lockup, the wordmark sits one ring radius to the right of the mark, optically centred on the rings.
