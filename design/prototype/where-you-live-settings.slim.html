Changing where you live

A settings row shows the country, and the city when the member lives abroad. The change screen uses the same five options. On a paid plan, one plain line says what happens to it. Changes are allowed once every 30 days; inside that window the screen states the next date in a neutral note and the options are read-only. No red, no error styling.

Hand-off: a country outside Nigeria continues into the existing city picker (city-picker.html) in its settings mode, unchanged. Tap a city here to see the saved state.
